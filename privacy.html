Kiube – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Developer: Gilberto Amaral · Draft, pending legal review

The developer does not collect any information about you.

No account, no data server, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

In short

Kiube keeps your progress on your device. There is no account, and the developer does not receive your data.

What the app stores

Your progress in the lessons (stages completed, badges and the dates you earned them, best challenge times, days in a row), your answer to “is your cube solved or scrambled”, and your language and display choices. All of it is stored only on your device.

No account, no data collection

Kiube has no sign-up and no server that receives your information. We cannot see your progress, and nothing is sold or shared.

Camera

This version does not use the camera. A future version may offer camera help to recognise which step your cube is at. If it does, the plan is for the images to be processed on your device and not sent anywhere, and this policy will be updated before it ships.

Advertising and tracking

This version has no advertising and no analytics, and does not track you. A free version with ads and a paid ad-free version are planned. If ads are added, the ad provider (for example Google AdMob) may use device identifiers under its own policy; Kiube will ask for your consent where the law requires, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Kiube is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Because the data stays on your device, you control it: uninstall the app and everything is removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.

Contact

Questions: contato@amaral.pro
