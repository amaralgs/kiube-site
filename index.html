The method

Seven steps to solved.

A layer-by-layer method that beginners can follow without memorising everything at once.

  1. Step 1White cross
  2. Step 2White corners
  3. Step 3Middle layer
  4. Step 4Yellow cross
  5. Step 5Yellow edges
  6. Step 6Position the corners
  7. Step 7Orient the corners

Learn the language of moves

Moves you can read.

Every move is a turn of one face. Kiube teaches them with animations before you use them, so a sequence like this stops looking like code:

RUR'U' Right, Up, Right back, Up back: one of the first sequences you will learn.
